About, Contact & Legal

About RateDost

RateDost Pakistan ke government aur market rates ko ek jagah samajhne aur compare karne ka simple platform hai. RateDost is an independent information platform and is not affiliated with any government department unless explicitly stated.

Founder & Owner: Bilawal Khan Niazi

Contact

Sawal ya feedback ke liye admin panel se ya apne platform ke listed contact channel se raabta karein.

Privacy Policy

RateDost sirf zaroori maloomat store karta hai — jaise apki city selection aur agar aap community report submit karein to us mein di gayi maloomat. Hum aap ka data kisi teesre party ko nahi bechte.

Terms of Use

RateDost par diye gaye rates general maloomat ke liye hain. In par mabni koi bhi financial ya business decision lene se pehle official source se tasdeeq zaroor karein.

Disclaimer

Demo/manual data is app mein use ho sakta hai jab tak live official source connect na ho. RateDost khud ba khud koi official government source nahi hai. Market/community reports guaranteed nahi hain aur prices kabhi bhi badal sakte hain. Important prices verify karne ke liye hamesha official source check karein.
